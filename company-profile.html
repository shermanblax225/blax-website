Who We Are

BLAX Mobility Sdn Bhd provides premium pre-booked chauffeur transportation for private customers, business travellers, corporate clients and airport passengers across Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley.

What We Do

Our core services include KLIA and KLIA2 airport transfers, premium chauffeur service, executive and corporate transportation, and private pre-booked city rides.

Our Service Standard

We focus on punctuality, professional communication, comfortable vehicles and dependable service from the moment a booking is confirmed until the passenger reaches the destination.

Corporate Transportation

BLAX supports executive movements, visiting guests, airport transfers, business meetings and recurring corporate transportation requirements.

Our Fleet

Our service options include Mercedes-Benz GLC, Toyota Camry, Toyota Alphard and Hyundai Staria, allowing us to serve individual executives, families and groups.

Service Coverage

Kuala Lumpur, Klang Valley, KLIA and KLIA2, with pre-booking for planned journeys.

Our Commitment

BLAX aims to provide more than transportation. We want every passenger to experience an organised, comfortable and professional journey that reflects the premium standard of our brand.
